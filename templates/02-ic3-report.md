# 2. FBI IC3 report

**Where:** [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov/) → File a Complaint. US residents, or crimes involving US companies.
**File this first.** Save the confirmation number and the PDF copy it offers at the end; you can't get the PDF later.

## Section by section

**Victim information:** your real name and contact details.

**Financial transactions:** add one entry per stolen transaction from your [evidence log](01-evidence-log.md):
- Type: *cryptocurrency*
- Amount and date
- Sending address (yours) and receiving address (the attacker's)
- Transaction hash

**Subject (the attacker):** you usually don't have a name. Enter attacker wallet addresses and any website, app, or username involved.

**Description:** paste and fill this in:

> I own the wallet(s) [ADDRESSES] on [NETWORKS]. Unknown attackers obtained my wallet key(s). Starting around [DATE], [funds were transferred out / a sweeper was attached that redirects anything sent to the wallet]. I did not make any outgoing transactions after [DATE].
>
> **How it may have happened:** [e.g., a fake support contact, a website I connected to, a download, unknown]. *(Say "unknown" if you don't know.)*
>
> **Attacker addresses:**
> - [ADDRESS]: [what it did], transaction [HASH]
> - [ADDRESS]: [what it did], transaction [HASH]
>
> **Lead for investigators:** attacker address [ADDRESS] was first funded on [DATE] by [EXCHANGE] in transaction [HASH]. Exchanges verify identity, so I ask that investigators request the account holder's information for that withdrawal.
>
> **Confirmed loss:** [AMOUNT], transactions [HASHES].
> **Additionally inaccessible:** [TOKENS still in the compromised wallet, approximate value, and why: frozen / swept].
>
> I have an evidence log with all addresses and transaction hashes and can provide it on request.

## Tips
- **Only list confirmed losses as losses.** Put stuck tokens under "inaccessible."
- No hash, no claim. If you aren't sure about something, write *unconfirmed*.
- IC3 rarely replies. That's normal. Its number is what other reports and exchanges ask for.
