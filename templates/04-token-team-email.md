# 4. Email to a token's project team (frozen or blacklisted tokens)

**Use only contact details from the project's official website.** Scammers pose as project teams in Discord and Telegram DMs.

**Subject:** Recovery request: compromised wallet [SHORT ADDRESS, e.g. 0x1234…abcd], [AMOUNT] [TOKEN]

> Hello [PROJECT] team,
>
> My wallet **[FULL ADDRESS]** on [NETWORK] holds **[AMOUNT] [TOKEN]**. It is [blacklisted / frozen] in your contract [CONTRACT ADDRESS] (status verified on [DATE]: [how, e.g. "isBlacklisted returns true"]).
>
> The wallet's key has been compromised: [e.g., an attacker attached a sweeper on DATE, transaction HASH]. I've filed an FBI IC3 complaint, number [NUMBER].
>
> I'd like to recover the tokens to a new wallet I control: **[NEW ADDRESS]**.
>
> **Important:** please don't simply unfreeze the old wallet. The attacker also holds its key and has [pre-set approvals / a sweeper] ready to drain it the moment it's unfrozen. The unfreeze and the transfer need to happen together in one private transaction bundle. I have a tested rescue plan and can share the details with your technical team.
>
> Could you:
> 1. Tell me why the wallet was [blacklisted / frozen], and
> 2. Let me know whether you can help with a coordinated recovery, and who on your team I should work with?
>
> I can verify ownership by signing a message with [the old wallet / a method you choose]. I will never share a private key or recovery phrase, and I understand you will never ask for one.
>
> Thank you,
> [NAME]

## Follow-up (after 5–7 days with no reply)

> Following up on my email of [DATE] about the compromised wallet [ADDRESS] holding [AMOUNT] [TOKEN]. Could you confirm it was received and tell me who is handling it? I've attached my original message below.

Try every official channel they list (email, website form, official X account). Keep a record of each in your [evidence log](01-evidence-log.md).
