# Templates

Fill-in-the-blank messages and forms for wallet theft victims, and for the people helping them. They come from a real case, cleaned of personal details.

**Before you use any of them:**
- **Never put your recovery phrase or private key in any of these.** No form, report, or support agent needs it.
- Replace everything in `[BRACKETS]`. Delete lines that don't apply.
- **Only state what you can prove.** Attach an address or transaction hash to every claim, and mark anything you're unsure of as *unconfirmed*.
