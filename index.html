How to read results. Checked live means read from the blockchain just now. Pattern match means the attached code looks like known sweeper code; that's strong evidence, not proof. A clean result means no sweeper is attached; it can't prove your key is safe.

Your address is sent to public blockchain servers (RPC providers) to read data. Nothing is stored by this page. Open source; check the code yourself.

Recovery scam warning: anyone who contacts you offering to "recover" or "unlock" funds for an upfront fee is almost certainly a scammer.
