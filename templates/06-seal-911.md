# 6. SEAL 911 message (active attacks)

SEAL (Security Alliance) is a group of volunteer security responders. **Use only the contact link on their official site, [securityalliance.org](https://securityalliance.org/).** Impostors use names like "SEAL support" in DMs.

SEAL 911 is best for attacks that are **still happening**, especially when funds are still at risk. Keep the first message short and complete:

> **Active wallet compromise, funds still at risk.**
> - Network: [NETWORK]
> - Victim wallet: [ADDRESS]
> - What's happening: [sweeper attached, transaction HASH / attacker draining tokens]
> - Still inside: [AMOUNT TOKEN] (frozen / not frozen)
> - Attacker addresses: [ADDRESSES]
> - Attacker funded by: [EXCHANGE, transaction HASH] (if known)
> - Reports filed: IC3 [NUMBER]
> - Need: [help with a private rescue bundle / contact with the token team / tracing]
> - Safe destination wallet: [NEW ADDRESS]
>
> I will never share a private key or recovery phrase.

They are volunteers and may not answer every request. Keep working through the other steps while you wait.
