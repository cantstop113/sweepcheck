# 8. CFPB complaint (US)

**Where:** [consumerfinance.gov/complaint](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/). Free. The company must respond to you in writing, usually within about 15 days.

**Use it when:** a payment app or bank ignores your dispute, keeps sending scripts, or denies it without giving you the documents it relied on.

## What to choose
- **Product:** pick the closest match for the app (for example, a mobile or digital wallet, or money transfer).
- **Issue:** the closest match for unauthorized transactions or a problem with how your dispute was handled.

## What happened

> On [DATE(S)], [NUMBER] unauthorized transfers totaling [$] were made from my [APP] account. I did not make or authorize them. I disputed them in writing through [APP]'s support chat on [DATE], case [NUMBER].
>
> [APP] [has not completed an investigation / denied my claim on DATE without providing the documents it relied on / has only sent scripted replies].
>
> I requested the device, IP address, location, and authentication records for each transaction on [DATE] and have not received them.

## What you want them to do

> Complete a proper investigation under Regulation E, provide the documents relied on, preserve all records, and refund the unauthorized transfers of [$].

Attach screenshots of your dispute chat and denial. Remove your full account number first.
