# 5. Wallet or exchange support: getting a real escalation

Use in-app support or the company's official website only, never someone who DMs you.

## Opening message

> Hi. My wallet [ADDRESS] was compromised on [DATE]. I'm not asking you to reverse transactions. I'm reporting these attacker addresses so your security team can flag them and warn other users:
>
> - [ADDRESS]: [what it did], transaction [HASH]
> - [ADDRESS]: [what it did], transaction [HASH]
>
> FBI IC3 complaint [NUMBER]. Please escalate this to your security team and give me a ticket number.

## When you get a script or a non-answer

> To be clear, I'm not asking you to reverse anything. I'm asking you to escalate [these addresses / my case] to [your security team / your dispute team]. Please give me:
> 1. The ticket number,
> 2. The team it went to, and
> 3. When I'll hear back.
>
> If you can't escalate it, please tell me why in writing.

## When they say "I've reported it on my side"

> Thanks. So I can follow up, what's the ticket or reference number, and which team received it?

**Keep it calm and short.** Save a copy of the chat (screenshot or export) and log the ticket number in your [evidence log](01-evidence-log.md).
