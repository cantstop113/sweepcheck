# The Squad

**Victim-first help for people whose crypto wallets were drained, swept, or frozen.**

> **Status: getting started.** We're not taking new cases yet. Until we are, start with [SweepCheck](https://cantstop113.github.io/sweepcheck/) and the [Wallet Drained? Your First 24 Hours](README.md#wallet-drained-your-first-24-hours) guide. Both are free.

## Why we exist

Security teams focus on big protocol hacks. Support desks answer with scripts. "Recovery experts" prey on victims. Someone whose wallet key was stolen gets told the wallet is "toast" and is left on their own.

We exist for that person. No victim should face the first 24 hours alone.

## Who we help

Individual people, especially those without technical skills, without money for professionals, or without anyone to call. **Seniors, veterans, people with disabilities, and first-time crypto users come first.**

## What we do

| | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Triage** | What happened, what's safe, and what's gone, in plain words. |
| **Stop the bleeding** | Clear first-hour steps so you don't lose more, including to "send gas money" traps. |
| **Evidence and reports** | We trace the theft and prepare your IC3, Chainabuse, and block explorer reports, ready to file. |
| **Escalation** | We write to projects, wallet support, and exchanges with the evidence attached, and push for a real answer. |
| **Rescue planning** | If tokens are still in the wallet, we plan a private rescue. **You sign on your own device.** |
| **Fresh start** | Help setting up a new wallet safely: large print, phone zoom, one step at a time. |
| **Aftercare** | Warnings about recovery scams and fake support, and a check-in afterward. |
| **Public warnings** | We share attack patterns and scam sites, never victim details. |

## Our promises

These keep us safe, legal, and different from the scammers. They are not negotiable.

1. **We never ask for your recovery phrase or private key.** Not ever. Anyone who asks is not us.
2. **We never DM you first.** If "the Squad" messages you out of nowhere, it's a scam.
3. **Free to victims.** No upfront fees, no "recovery fees," no percentage.
4. **No hack-back.** We don't touch attacker wallets or try to "steal it back." We trace and report. Law enforcement acts.
5. **Your permission, in writing.** We act only for the wallet owner, or someone they authorize in writing.
6. **Honest about what we know.** Every finding is labeled *verified*, *unconfirmed*, or *our best guess*. We never promise recovery.
7. **Your privacy first.** Your name, wallet, and story stay private unless you say otherwise.
8. **We know our limits.** If you need a lawyer or law enforcement, we say so and point you there.
9. **Receipts.** Every case ends with a written record of what we found, what we filed, and what's still open.

## No dead ends

Our first case taught us what bad support feels like: boilerplate answers, replies to questions nobody asked, and "we've reported it" with no ticket and no timeline. So every Squad member follows these rules in every conversation:

1. **Answer the question you asked.** We restate it in one line before we reply.
2. **Never just "can't."** Every "we can't" comes with a "here's what we can."
3. **We read your evidence.** If you send transaction links, our reply uses them.
4. **Escalation is your right.** Ask us to escalate and we will, or we'll tell you in writing why not. Every escalation gets an owner and a deadline.
5. **No dead ends.** We never say there's no way to reach whoever can decide your case. We route it and give you a receipt.
6. **No scripts for scared people.** Every reply is written to you.

## Funding

Never from victims. We plan to use public-goods grants, individual donors, and sponsors. Sponsors get no say in cases and no access to victim data. We'll publish where money comes from and where it goes. *(No funding is secured yet.)*
