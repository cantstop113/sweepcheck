Is someone sweeping your wallet?

Paste a wallet address. In seconds you'll see, in plain words, whether thieves have attached a "sweeper" that steals every coin you send in, and what to do next.

We never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Nobody legitimate ever will. This page only reads public blockchain data.

Heads up: fake "Binance refund" scams (Nov 1, 2026)

The FBI says North Korea's TraderTraitor group stole about $6.3 billion from Binance on October 29. Investigators at Elliptic found the thieves used the same trick this page checks for: an EIP-7702 "sweeper" attached to a Binance wallet.

Scammers will now pose as Binance support, offering refunds or "compensation."

  • Binance will not DM you first, on any app.
  • No refund needs your recovery phrase, your private key, a wallet connection, or a "fee."
  • Only trust news on binance.com itself, typed in by hand.
