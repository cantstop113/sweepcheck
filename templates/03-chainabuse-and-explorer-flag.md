# 3. Chainabuse and block explorer flags

These warn exchanges, wallets, and other people away from the attacker. Do them after IC3 so you can include its number.

## Chainabuse

**Where:** [chainabuse.com](https://www.chainabuse.com/) → Report Scam.

- **Category:** pick the closest (for a stolen key or sweeper, usually *Hack* or *Other*).
- **Addresses:** each attacker address, with the right network.
- **Description:**

> Attacker address in a wallet theft. My wallet [YOUR ADDRESS] on [NETWORK] was compromised around [DATE]. This address [received stolen funds / is the sweeper contract that forwards incoming coins / was given an unauthorized approval] in transaction [HASH]. FBI IC3 complaint [NUMBER].

- **Loss:** confirmed amount only.

## Block explorer "Report / Flag Address"

**Where:** open the attacker's address on the explorer (bscscan.com, basescan.org, etherscan.io) → **More** or **⋯** → **Report/Flag Address**. You may need a free explorer account.

> This address was used to steal from my wallet [YOUR ADDRESS] on [DATE]: [what it did], transaction [HASH]. Please label it as a phishing/hack address. FBI IC3 complaint [NUMBER].

**Don't** flag an exchange's own deposit or hot wallet. Note it in your IC3 report instead.
